## How to use these

- **For email**: open either file on GitHub, copy the rendered content, and
  paste into Outlook. Markdown tables and links carry over cleanly.
- **For PDF / Word**: download the raw file, paste into a Word doc, apply
  your house style. Most of the structure (headings, tables) will survive.
- **For meetings**: the TL;DR at the top of `business-case-upgrade.md` is
  designed to be read aloud in under 60 seconds.
